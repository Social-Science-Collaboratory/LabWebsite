We plan on reviewing applications for 0–1 PhD students to join the team starting Fall 2026.

PhD students can usually expect a full tuition remission and a small stipend for 5 years.

Information about the application process, the program, and life at UF, is available here. Questions should be addressed to psych-info@ufl.edu.

To ensure fairness, we do not meet with or provide applicants with feedback. If you have reviewed information about our research group and believe there is a strong match with your interests, we encourage you to apply.

If/when paid and volunteer positions open in the lab, we will post them here.

Email is acceptable, but please be mindful of email overload.
