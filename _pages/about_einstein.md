**I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Florida, where I direct the Social Science Collaboratory: a group dedicated to building and understanding big team science initiatives. **


Much of my work focuses on collective intelligence – both human and artificial. For example, I founded the [Many Smiles Collaboration](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-022-01458-9) and the [Emotion Physiology and Experience Collaboration](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.241778). Through global experimentation and crowdsourced machine learning, these collaborations helped document the physiological basis of emotional consciousness. Through this kind of work, I have more broadly studied the [rise](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/b3mq6_v1), [benefits](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-022-00150-2), and [challenges](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.ado7070) of big teams across the history of science – ranging from the historic Manhattan Project to the modern AI Race.
